How we verify →
Every figure is checked against the chain before it ships:
  • Genesis. Total supply reconciles to the original mint — 420,000,069 TOKE, 71 equal genesis airdrops.
  • Balances. The sum of every wallet's balance equals on-chain supply, to the grain.
  • Independent cross-check. Holder count and supply are re-derived from a direct on-chain account scan on a public RPC — a provider independent of our data pipeline — and must match exactly.
  • Transfer flow. Each wallet's net of all transfers in and out is reconciled against its current balance, to within a small, documented tolerance on legacy DAO/vault accounts.
Data is published only when these checks pass. The banner above reflects the current verification state.
